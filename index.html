What it does not do

A scanned statement, a photograph of a page, cannot be read. Ask your bank for the text PDF, which is the one they email. Reading text messages is not possible in an app that lives in the browser.

Settings

Privacy, storage and your data.

Your money stays on this phone. Expenses and bank statements never touch a server. There is no account, no cloud and no analytics.

Staying on the phone is not a backup. If you clear your browser data, remove the app, or the phone runs short of space, these records can be erased. Keep a backup file somewhere you trust.
