How it will work

Once a month you pick the PDF your bank sends. It is read entirely on this phone and nothing leaves the device. A summary tells you how many lines were recognised automatically and asks you only about the handful it was unsure of. Entries you added by hand get matched, never deleted.

Where this stands

Statement import is the next thing being built, starting with Commercial Bank. A bank is only listed as supported after a real sample statement has been parsed correctly. Until then, everything you add by hand is kept as provisional and will reconcile against the first statement you bring in.
